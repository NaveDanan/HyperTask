# Project conventions

- The product is named HyperTask and was developed by NaveDanan. Preserve upstream copyright and license notices.
- Prefix the names of new product modules and widgets with `Hyper` (for example, `HyperCalendar` or `HyperWhiteboard`).
- Keep existing protocol identifiers, data paths, and upstream dependency names compatible unless a migration is implemented.
- For workloads on the MMEMS K3s cluster, follow the `mmems-cluster-images` skill.
